The RRULE alone cannot show this, so `[FREQ=MONTHLY;BYMONTHDAY=27]` reads as a fixed 27th-of-the-month task when it may not be one. - [x] **Text-field caps set to 60,000, TickTick's real limit is 164,130 (2026-08-19).** The `content` and `description` caps in `tools/inputs.py` are this server's own validation rules, and 60,000 is the operator's chosen value for all four fields (create and update, content and description).
